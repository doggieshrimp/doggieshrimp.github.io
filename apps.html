Nouri

Android app · web version live

A free, ad-free calorie and macro tracker, styled like the nutrition-facts label on food packaging. Log what you eat in a few taps and see what's left of your daily goal at a glance.

Track your day

Daily calorie goal with a remaining or over indicator, plus protein, carbs and fat. Day-by-day history.

Find food fast

Search USDA FoodData Central, scan barcodes on Android, or add your own foods. Recent foods and saved meals are one tap away.

Easy to read

Dyslexia-friendly settings based on the British Dyslexia Association style guide: tinted backgrounds, 4 fonts including OpenDyslexic, larger text and extra spacing.

Private by design

Your log stays on your device, with backup export and import. The goal calculator never stores your height, weight or age.

StudyXP

In development

A study app that turns revision into a game. Instead of a boring to-do list, you complete quests, earn XP and level up, so there's always a reason to do one more session.

Quests

Timed focus quests with a built-in study timer, daily checkmark quests, and your own custom quests.

XP & levels

Every quest earns XP towards your next level, with a daily streak to keep you coming back.

Study Buddy

A pet to name, feed and look after, with a bubble-popping mini-game that earns bonus XP.

Stats

See your progress over time. Everything is saved on your device.
