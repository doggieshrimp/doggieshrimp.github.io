About

A bit about me

I'm a web developer and 3D modeller who likes making things people can actually use: a game you can open and play in seconds, a tool that does one job well, a site that helps a local business get customers, or an app that makes studying less of a chore.

On the web, I write everything by hand in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, from collision physics for a game to real encryption in the browser. For apps I use React with Capacitor and Flutter, and for 3D I model, pose and render in Blender.
